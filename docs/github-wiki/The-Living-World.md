# The living world

WorldForge is built around a world that keeps pressure and memory when the player looks away.

## NPCs

NPCs use different levels of persistence.

- Key NPCs can have goals, memories, private knowledge, and ongoing plans.
- Persistent NPCs remain part of the world but may not act independently every turn.
- Temporary NPCs can appear for a scene and fade unless they become important.

If you keep interacting with someone, they may become more important over time.

## Factions

Factions are groups with goals, resources, territory, orders, reports, and pressure.

They might:

- Investigate an event.
- Send people somewhere.
- Hide evidence.
- Spread a rumor.
- Prepare an operation.
- React to your public actions.
- Fight each other while you are elsewhere.

You do not need to join a faction for factions to matter.

## Rumors and reports

The game can distinguish between:

- Something that really happened.
- Something someone believes.
- Something someone reported.
- Something the player heard as a rumor.
- Something hidden from the player.

This matters because a rumor can be useful without being true.

## Hidden information

WorldForge gives the narrator player-visible material instead of the whole hidden state.

Your character may need a real path to learn hidden information:

- Witness it.
- Ask the right person.
- Find a document.
- Use a relevant ability.
- Infer it from clues.
- Receive a report from someone who knows.

If you guess a secret correctly, the game should not automatically confirm it unless your character has evidence.

## Consequences

Consequences can be immediate or delayed.

You might:

- Make an enemy.
- Start a rumor.
- Leave evidence.
- Give a faction a reason to move.
- Cause an NPC to change plans.
- Create pressure that returns later.

This is why small choices can matter in long play.

## Simulation scope

WorldForge focuses attention where it matters: visible scenes, important NPCs, active factions, unresolved threads, and consequences that can reach the player.

This keeps turns playable without pretending to simulate every person at every second.
